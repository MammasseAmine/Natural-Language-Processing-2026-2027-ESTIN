What We Learned

NLP has moved from hand-crafted rules → probabilities → learned representations → massive, general-purpose models. Each era built on the last. Today, we use a mix of all three – rule-based tools for small jobs, statistical thinking for everything, and neural networks for the heavy lifting. The future is multimodal, agentic, and increasingly open. But the core challenge remains the same: turning the rich, messy beauty of human language into something a machine can truly understand.
