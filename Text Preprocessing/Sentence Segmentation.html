Sentence Segmentation

Sentence Boundary Detection (SBD)
What is Sentence Segmentation?

Sentence Segmentation (also called Sentence Boundary Detection or SBD) is the task of splitting a chunk of text into individual sentences. It's the very first step in almost any NLP pipeline – and it's trickier than it looks.

Example
Input: Dr. Smith arrived at 8 a.m. He started the lecture immediately. The students were ready.
  • 1. Dr. Smith arrived at 8 a.m.
  • 2. He started the lecture immediately.
  • 3. The students were ready.

The system correctly splits at the sentence boundaries – even with Dr. and a.m. in the first sentence.

What Defines a Sentence?

In written language, sentences are usually separated by one of these punctuation marks:

.
Period
"The lecture is starting."
?
Question Mark
"Are you ready?"
!
Exclamation Mark
"Excellent work!"

These punctuation marks are called sentence boundary markers.

The Sentence Boundary Detection Problem

Detecting sentence boundaries seems simple – but it's actually full of traps. The same punctuation mark can mean different things depending on context.

Abbreviation
Dr. Ahmed teaches NLP.
The period after "Dr." is not a sentence end.
Decimal Number
The accuracy reached 98.7%.
The period is part of the number.
URL
Visit www.example.com for details.
Periods inside URLs are not boundaries.
Email
Contact me at professor@univ.edu.
The period in ".edu" is not a boundary.
Initials
A. Einstein developed the theory.
Initials use periods but don't end sentences.
Acronym
The meeting was held at the U.N.
Periods inside acronyms are not boundaries.
Ellipsis
Well... I am not sure.
Ellipsis has multiple dots but is not a boundary.
Quoted Sentence
She said, "I will come tomorrow."
The period inside quotes may or may not be the boundary.
Improved Rule-Based Systems

The simplest way to handle ambiguity is to use an abbreviation dictionary (exception list). Common abbreviations include:

Dr. Prof. Mr. Mrs. etc. e.g. i.e. Ms. Jr. Sr.
Check token In abbreviation list? Yes → Do NOT split or No → Split
Rule-Based Example
Input: Dr. Ahmed teaches NLP. Students enjoy the class.
  • 1. Dr. Ahmed teaches NLP.
  • 2. Students enjoy the class.

The rule-based system sees Dr. in its dictionary and skips the split.

Advantages

  • Easy to implement
  • Fast execution
  • Interpretable & transparent

Disadvantages

  • Language-dependent
  • Manual maintenance required
  • Poor scalability
Machine Learning Approaches

ML models treat sentence segmentation as a binary classification problem. For each punctuation mark, the model asks: "Is this a sentence boundary?"

Statistical Models

Before deep learning, researchers used statistical models to learn boundary patterns from annotated text (corpora). Popular models included:

Naive Bayes Decision Trees Max Entropy Conditional Random Fields (CRFs)

These models learn from labeled data – where humans have already marked which periods are boundaries and which are not.

Neural Network Approaches

Modern NLP uses neural networks that can learn complex patterns from raw text.

Recurrent (RNN)

Processes text from left to right. Uses surrounding context to decide if a period is a boundary.

Input: "Dr. Ahmed arrived."

LSTM

Captures long-range dependencies – remembers information from many words back.

Better context understanding

Bidirectional LSTM

Reads text left → right and right → left. Sees the full context.

Improves boundary detection accuracy

Transformer-Based Sentence Segmentation

Transformers are the current state-of-the-art. They process all words at once and understand context better than any previous approach.

BERT RoBERTa DistilBERT
How Transformers Help

Input: "Dr. Ahmed arrived at 9 a.m. The lecture began."

The transformer looks at the full context around a.m. and understands it's not the final sentence boundary – because "The lecture began" follows.

Why It Matters Downstream

Sentence boundaries are the unit of analysis for almost the entire NLP stack. A single wrong split can break everything downstream.

Parsing & POS Tagging

Parsers are trained on single sentences. Merge two sentences → grammar breaks. Split one in half → tree incomplete.

Machine Translation

MT systems translate sentence-by-sentence. A wrong split scrambles word order and can drop sentences.

Summarization & IE

Extractive summarizers select whole sentences. Relation extraction assumes arguments live inside one sentence.

Text-to-Speech

Prosody (pause, intonation) is planned per sentence. Merge two sentences → speech runs on without a breath.

Readability Metrics

Average sentence length is a key readability feature. Unreliable segmentation makes this metric meaningless.

Key Takeaway

Sentence Segmentation is the unsung hero of NLP. It's the first step in every pipeline, and getting it right matters more than you'd think. From simple abbreviation lists to state-of-the-art transformers – the goal is the same: teach machines where one sentence ends and the next begins.
